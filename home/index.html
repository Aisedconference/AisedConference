Introduction

AiSED International Conference 2026.

Harnessing AI. Empowering Innovation. Advancing Sustainable Futures.

The AiSED International Conference 2026 brings together policymakers, academics, entrepreneurs, diplomats, industry leaders and experts from around the world to explore how Artificial Intelligence, Sustainable Entrepreneurship and Diplomacy can build a more inclusive, resilient and future-ready world.

Held in Shah Alam, Selangor, the conference provides a platform for sharing knowledge, developing partnerships and exchanging ideas on technology, sustainability and international cooperation.

The conference is hosted by AiSED, with Asia e University and Gaine Academy as Joint Organisers. It is proudly held under the patronage of His Highness The Raja Muda of Selangor Tengku Amir Shah ibni Sultan Sharafuddin Idris Shah Alhaj, Patron of AiSED.

The programme will feature keynote speeches, plenary discussions, research paper presentations, industry forums, workshops and networking opportunities with established experts and emerging leaders from around the world.
